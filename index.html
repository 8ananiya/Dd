Read this first
  • Wear headphones — that's the only configuration where this behaves like real ANC (mic outside, anti-noise at your ear).
  • It works best on steady low-frequency sound: fans, engines, road rumble, air conditioning. Speech and music will not cancel.
  • If you hear a rising whistle, hit stop or lower intensity — that's acoustic feedback.
  • Browser audio latency (10–30 ms) limits cancellation to roughly 30–80 Hz. Real ANC chips run at under 1 ms. This is a demo of the principle, not a replacement for hardware.
