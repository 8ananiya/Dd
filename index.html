How it works

  • Binaural beats — two tones, one per ear, offset by a few Hz. Your brainstem perceives the difference as a rhythmic pulse.
  • Isochronic pulses — one tone gated on/off at the target rate. Works without headphones.
  • Carrier tones — Solfeggio-adjacent frequencies (396, 528, 639, 852 Hz) chosen per state, layered as a soft pad.
  • Ambient noise — pink or brown noise blended under the tones.
  • Hypnosis — a sequenced 4-stage descent through alpha → theta → deep theta → suggestion, with automatic frequency shifts.

Safety

  • Not a medical device. No clinical claim is made.
  • Do not use while driving or operating machinery.
  • Skip if you have epilepsy, a seizure disorder, or a pacemaker — rhythmic audio is a known trigger for some people.
  • Stop if you feel dizzy, anxious, or headachy. Best at 40–60 dB, not loud.
  • Binaural beats need headphones. Iso and pad work on speakers.
  • The hypnosis mode is a soundscape only — no verbal suggestion or guided meditation is included.
