Why this is not "real" ANC
  • Big-tech ANC uses two mics (feedforward + feedback) and a chip running at <1 ms latency. Browser audio is 10–30 ms, which caps cancellation at ~25 Hz.
  • With one mic, only feedback ANC is mathematically valid. That's what this uses: y = −W · e_past, NLMS-adapted.
  • Without headphones this self-oscillates instantly. The howl guard limits it, but headphones are the correct setup.
  • It cancels steady low-frequency rumble (fans, engines, HVAC). It cannot cancel speech, music, or transients — no ANC can.
