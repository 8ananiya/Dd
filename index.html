Using a headset microphone
  • Select your headset mic from the input device dropdown above. The badge turns green when the system detects a headset.
  • Bluetooth headsets may show as "Hands-Free AG Audio" — that's the mic channel, but it's low quality and has high latency. USB or 3.5mm headsets are preferred.
  • If you plug in a headset after opening the page, tap re-scan (↻) to refresh the device list.
  • Browser latency (10–30 ms) limits cancellation to ~25 Hz. This is the real FxLMS algorithm, just without the dedicated DSP silicon.
